About the course

Think clearly. Solve efficiently.

A structured introduction to creating, understanding, and evaluating algorithms.

⌁

Analyze complexity

Understand asymptotic notation, growth rates, recurrences, and practical trade-offs.

◇

Design algorithms

Use divide-and-conquer, greedy reasoning, and dynamic programming to solve problems.

✓

Reason rigorously

Establish correctness, compare approaches, and select appropriate techniques.
